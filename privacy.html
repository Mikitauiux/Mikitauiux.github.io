Privacy Policy

App: ThreadsUIUX · Last updated: November 24, 2026

ThreadsUIUX is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual designer (Threads: @mikitauiux). It searches public Threads posts for requests for UI/UX and product design work and sends matching posts to its operator in a private Telegram chat.

What data the app accesses

  • The operator's own Threads profile ID and username (permission threads_basic), used only to authenticate.
  • Public Threads posts returned by keyword search (permission threads_keyword_search): post ID, text, permalink, timestamp and author username.

How the data is used

  • Posts are filtered by keywords to find design job requests.
  • Matching posts are forwarded to the operator's private Telegram chat so the operator can read them and reply manually on Threads.
  • The app does not post, like, follow or send messages on anyone's behalf.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to build profiles of people.

Storage and retention

The app runs on the operator's personal computer. It keeps a local database of post IDs it has already processed, to avoid duplicates, and the text of posts forwarded to the operator. This data is not published or transferred anywhere except the operator's own Telegram chat. Stored posts are deleted on request (see below) and can be wiped at any time by deleting the local database.

Your rights and data deletion

If your public Threads post was processed by the app and you want it removed, follow the data deletion instructions. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Contact

Send a direct message to @mikitauiux on Threads.
