Data Deletion Instructions

App: ThreadsUIUX · Last updated: November 24, 2026

ThreadsUIUX stores only the IDs of public Threads posts it has processed and the text of posts forwarded to its operator. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @mikitauiux on Threads with the subject “ThreadsUIUX data deletion”.
  2. Include your Threads username and, if you have them, links to the posts concerned.
  3. All stored data related to your posts will be deleted within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation reply.

Revoking access

The app accesses only the operator's own Threads account. Anyone who authorized ThreadsUIUX can revoke access at any time in Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → Active → ThreadsUIUX → Remove.
